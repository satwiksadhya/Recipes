Indian Style Basmati Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Rinse rice until water runs clear.
  3. Heat oil and add cumin seeds until they splutter.
  4. Add rice and sauté briefly.
  5. Add water and salt, bring to boil.
  6. Cover and simmer until rice is cooked and fluffy.
