Mom's Chocolate Zucchini Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grated zucchini
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F).
  3. Cream butter and sugar, then add egg and vanilla.
  4. Mix in zucchini, then dry ingredients.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop spoonfuls onto baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes.
