Sriracha Honey Brussels Sprouts

Ingredients

  • 1 pound Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1–2 tablespoons Sriracha sauce (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
  3. Toss Brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20–25 minutes until crispy.
  5. In a small bowl, mix honey, Sriracha, soy sauce, and lime juice.
  6. Drizzle sauce over roasted Brussels sprouts and toss to coat.
  7. Serve warm.
